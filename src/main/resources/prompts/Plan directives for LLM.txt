Personal Training Session Structure Directives
Use these directives when designing or analyzing workout sessions for maximum impact and efficiency:

PHASE 0: FITNESS LEVEL ASSESSMENT
Keep in mind the user experience level when designing a plan
For ex: Beginner level->Focus on movements which helps the trainee understand their muscle structure and how to use muscles to achieve work

PHASE 1: SESSION INITIATION
Allocate 5-10 minutes for dynamic movements (leg swings, arm circles, light cardio)
Prioritize movement-specific exercises that mirror upcoming workout patterns
Ensure blood flow enhancement, muscle temperature elevation, and nervous system activation
Focus on injury prevention and performance optimization

PHASE 2: MAIN WORKOUT STRUCTURE
Place compound movements (squats, deadlifts, bench press) at the beginning when energy is highest
Sequence technically demanding lifts before fatigue compromises form
Organize around movement patterns: push, pull, hinge, squat, rotation (not isolated muscle groups)
Group exercises using similar equipment to minimize transition time

Implement progressive overload through weight, reps, sets, or complexity increases
Balance push/pull movements and opposing muscle groups within the session

When combining cardio and strength training, perform strength training first
Utilize glycogen stores during lifting to promote fat utilization during subsequent cardio
Maintain workout flow and intensity through efficient exercise transitions

PHASE 3: SESSION COMPLETION
Conclude with 5-10 minutes of low-intensity activity
Include static stretching, breathing exercises, and mobility work
